### What I work on

- **Design for Reliability (DfR):** reliability program plans, target setting and allocation, DRBFM, de-rating, reliability prediction
- **Reliability testing:** HALT, ALT, RDT and ESS, from component to full system
- **Life-data and degradation analysis:** Weibull and other life distributions, Kaplan-Meier survival analysis, degradation modeling, Monte Carlo simulation
- **Physics of Failure:** thermal, humidity, vibration and shock models for PCBAs; lithium-ion battery degradation
- **Risk and root cause:** FMEA (B-/P-diagram based), FTA, FRACAS, 8D
- **Predictive reliability with ML:** failure prediction models, predictive health monitoring, reliability dashboards
- **Tools:** Python, R, Minitab, ReliaSoft, Excel, Tableau, SolidWorks

Applied across data-center infrastructure, consumer robotics, medical imaging, automotive electronics and consumer devices.
